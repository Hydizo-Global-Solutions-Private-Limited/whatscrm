MsgMagnet Mobile App (iOS & Android)

Native mobile companion built with React Native & Expo SDK 52. Seamless on-the-go card scanning, offline mode, and NFC sharing.

📱 Native Mobile Features
  • 📸 Viewfinder Camera & Edge Detection: Frame business cards perfectly with illuminated bounding box.
  • 📥 Offline Mode with SQLite: Keep scanning and adding tasks at events without internet; auto-syncs when online.
  • ⚡ Physical NFC Tag Writer: Write your digital business card directly onto NFC plastic cards or tags on tap.
  • 🎙️ Voice-to-CRM: Tap the mic and speak notes; Gemini AI transcribes and structures tasks automatically.
  • 🗺️ Geo-Networking Map: Plot your contacts with heat-coded pins on native Apple/Google Maps.
  • 🚀 Zero-Delay OTA Updates: Instantly update the app over-the-air via EAS without App Store delays.
